MOTIVE

The hardest component to capture.

Ash and dust; where we are birthed, and where we go. We grow in an effort to fade, decompose, and return to soil. But components of life are crafted, formed in the in-between moments, that give our spirits a reason to ramble, remember, and move forward.

Observation leaves us sightless; the more you explore, the more you realize that you've never truly seen. The world, the people, its purpose, and its meaning. The function of exploration is to uncover pieces of a larger mystery. To find ourselves, and to find the places, and people we meet.
